# Credits

## Datasets

**1. GritWorks — Free OCR Sample Dataset**
Source: https://gritworks.ai/resources/free-datasets/ocr
Purpose: Invoice, purchase order, credit memo and account statement extraction testing.
Note: Fictional sample documents. Check the source page for usage terms.

**2. FileDone — Sample Documents**
Source: https://filedone.ai/sample-files
Purpose: PDF, scanned invoice, receipt and bank statement extraction testing.
License: CC0 1.0 (Public Domain).

**3. Mindweave — US Bank Transactions Dataset**
Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/mindweave/bank-transactions-us
Purpose: Synthetic bank transaction and spending analytics testing.
License: CC BY-NC 4.0.

**4. UCI Machine Learning Repository — Online Retail**
Source: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/352/online-retail
Creator: Daqing Chen (2015).
Purpose: Revenue analysis, transaction trends and sales analytics testing.
License: CC BY 4.0.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.24432/C5BW33
